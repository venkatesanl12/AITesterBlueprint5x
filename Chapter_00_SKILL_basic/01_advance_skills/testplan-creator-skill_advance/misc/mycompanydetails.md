
📊 Walmart Data Ventures Overview

Walmart Data Ventures is a specialized commercial data and insights business unit within Walmart that monetizes first-party retail data to help suppliers, merchants, and brands make informed decisions.

💡 Core Platform & Evolution

• Scintilla Ecosystem: Formerly known as Walmart Luminate, the rebranded Scintilla serves as the primary commerce intelligence platform.
• Insights to Action: The platform bridges raw data and execution, streamlining workflows across inventory management, retail media, and in-store optimization.
• Granular Visibility: Provides suppliers with integrated, multi-dimensional views of customer behavior and business performance across the U.S., Mexico, and Canada.

📌 Key Focus Areas

• Supplier Collaboration: Empowers thousands of suppliers via annual events like Inspire to drive joint growth.
• Decision-Making: Replaces static reporting with dynamic, actionable intelligence to optimize product assortment and the customer shopping experience
